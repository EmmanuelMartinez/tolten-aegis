# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

This project adopts the **[Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)**.
By participating — issues, pull requests, discussions — you agree to uphold it.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project.
- Show empathy towards other community members.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, insults, derogatory comments, sexualised language or imagery, trolling,
publishing others' private information, or any conduct reasonably considered inappropriate
in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour to
[oe.martinez03@gmail.com](mailto:oe.martinez03@gmail.com). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and
fairly, and the reporter's privacy and security will be respected.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code and issues that do not comply,
and will communicate the reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.
